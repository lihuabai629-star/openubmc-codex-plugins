# Human readability and technical evidence

Read as the intended user before evaluating machine extractability. Retain
concrete reader-cost evidence rather than paragraph-length or SEO quotas.

| ID | Human criterion | Observable problem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H01 | Conclusion and information order | Reader cannot find the result, decision or applicable entry point |
| H02 | Reader context | A hidden permission, tool, term or product assumption prevents the task |
| H03 | Action and verification | A step lacks required input, observable output or a usable success check |
| H04 | Scannable structure | Titles, lists/tables and navigation conceal the sought information |
| H05 | Clear and economical prose | Ambiguous referents or irrelevant repetition cause a specific misreading |
| H06 | Coherent and complete path | Variables change between steps, prerequisites follow use, or a necessary branch is absent |
| H07 | Accuracy and consistency | A term, unit, value, identifier or example contradicts another statement |
| H08 | Honest evidence | A product/performance claim exceeds supporting source, test or observation |
| H09 | Progress and recall | A multi-step task gives no way to identify the current stage, completion or next step |

Record `Pass`, `Partial` or `Fail` for each applicable criterion with location,
observed effect and smallest useful correction. References need not include
estimated duration, beginner glossaries or tutorial summaries. Deduplicate with
completeness/command lanes when findings describe the same defect.

Then check extractability: heading/content alignment, stable entities and
identifiers, explicit units/versions, code/placeholder separation and claims linked
to relevant evidence. Preserve compact references usable by the intended reader.

For material claims retain the exact text/location, source/specification revision
or artifact identity, measurement method/conditions/units, applicability and
limitations. Check totals and before/after comparisons. Record missing evidence
instead of manufacturing numbers, citations, FAQ text or author biographies.
Prioritize corrections that unblock the reader or prevent a wrong action.
